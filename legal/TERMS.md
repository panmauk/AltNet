## 1. What AltNet is

AltNet is a peer-to-peer network for hosting websites and files under the
`.alt` namespace. AltNet Studio is the open-source application that connects
your computer to that network. **There is no central server**: content is
stored and served by participating nodes (people's computers). We operate an
account/registration service and an always-on bootstrap node, but we do not
host or control the content on the network.

## 5. Your responsibilities

**As a publisher:** When you register a `.alt` name and publish under it, you
are the publisher and are solely responsible for that content's legality. We
do not pre-screen the bytes of published content, but we review every name
request before approval and act on reports.

**As a node operator:** Running a node means your computer stores and serves
content-addressed pieces of other people's content. The content is chunked
and addressed by hash; **you do not see and cannot choose what files you
serve**. AltNet Studio ships a blocklist of known-bad content hashes (CSAM
and similar) that your node refuses to store or serve, and applies signed
revocations that purge taken-down content. You can stop being a node at any
time from the Node screen.

## 6. Moderation and notice-and-action

- We **review every `.alt` name request** before it goes live.
- **Anyone can report** a name via the in-app "Report a site" link or by
  emailing **abuse.report@panmox.org**. See `legal/TAKEDOWN.md` for the
  formal notice procedure (DMCA and EU DSA).
- We aim to **review reports within 72 hours**.
- If a report is upheld, the name is **revoked**: a cryptographically signed
  deletion record is broadcast so the content's chunks are purged from nodes
  across the network, and the name stops resolving.
- We will provide the affected publisher a brief statement of reasons where
  required by the EU Digital Services Act, and you may appeal (see §8).
